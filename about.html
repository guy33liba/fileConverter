About File Converter

A simple browser-based tool for converting images and PDFs without uploading your files to a server.

File Converter helps convert PNG, JPG, WebP, AVIF, and PDF files directly in your browser. PDF text extraction and summaries are generated locally from readable PDF text.

Use the free file converter for image and PDF conversions, or try the PDF summarizer when you need a quick summary from readable PDF text.
